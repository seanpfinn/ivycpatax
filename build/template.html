S-Corp salary vs. distributions

One profit. Two ways out.

S-Corp owners pay themselves two ways: a salary that carries payroll tax, and distributions that don't. Where you draw the line is the heart of S-Corp planning, and it has to be defensible.

  • Too low a salary invites IRS scrutiny.
  • Too high costs payroll tax you didn't need to pay.
  • The right number fits the work you actually do, and it's documented.
